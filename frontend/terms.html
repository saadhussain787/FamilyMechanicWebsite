NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN PURSUANT TO THE REPAIR AND STORAGE LIENS ACT, R.S.O. 1990, c. R.25 (RSLA):

Possessory & Non-Possessory Liens: My Choice Auto Service retains an immediate statutory repair and storage lien against every vehicle serviced for the full unpaid balance of authorized labor, parts, materials, and accrued storage fees.

48-Hour Vehicle Pickup Window: Upon completion of authorized repairs, Customer will be notified via phone, SMS, or email that the vehicle is ready for pickup. Customers must settle all outstanding invoices and retrieve the vehicle within forty-eight (48) hours of notification.

Storage Fees: Any vehicle remaining on our premises beyond the 48-hour pickup window without prior written agreement is subject to a commercial storage fee of \$50.00 CAD per calendar day.

Enforcement & Sale Rights: If repair invoices and storage charges remain unpaid after statutory notice periods mandated by the RSLA, My Choice Auto Service reserves the legal right to enforce its lien through seizure, retention, or public sale of the vehicle in accordance with Ontario law.

5. Parts Warranties & Workmanship Guarantee

We take immense pride in mechanical excellence. Our warranty structure is straightforward and honest:

90-Day / 5,000 km Labor Warranty
