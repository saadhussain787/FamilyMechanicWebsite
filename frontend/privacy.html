Legal & Compliance Shield

Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Certified: November 2026 | PIPEDA & Ontario Statutory Compliance

1. Commitment to Canadian Privacy Standards (PIPEDA)

My Choice Auto Service (“we”, “our”, or “us”), led by Certified Auto Technician Shafiq Patel, is committed to safeguarding the personal and vehicle diagnostic information of our customers across Scarborough and the Greater Toronto Area (GTA).

This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent practices regarding the collection, use, retention, and protection of your data in strict compliance with the Canadian federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable Ontario provincial consumer privacy laws.

2. Information We Collect

To perform honest, certified automotive repairs and manage client appointments, we collect only the necessary information:

Customer Identifiers

  • Full legal name
  • Telephone and mobile phone numbers
  • Email address for estimate confirmations
  • Residential or business billing address

Vehicle Telemetry & Records

  • Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)
  • License plate number and issuing jurisdiction
  • Vehicle year, make, model, trim, and engine displacement
  • Current odometer mileage readings
  • Diagnostic trouble codes (OBD-II scans) and mechanical service history

3. Purpose and Legal Grounds for Processing

We collect and utilize your personal and vehicle data solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • Appointment Scheduling & Lead Dispatch: Processing online appointment requests and notifying our technicians via secure cloud systems (AWS Lambda and Amazon SNS).
  • Diagnostic Accuracy & Parts Sourcing: Using your VIN to query exact OEM and aftermarket specification databases, safety recalls, and correct replacement components.
  • Repair Authorizations & Invoicing: Generating statutory written estimates and invoices in compliance with the Ontario Consumer Protection Act.
  • Service Reminders & Safety Notices: Informing you of manufacturer technical service bulletins (TSBs), safety inspections, or seasonal maintenance requirements.

4. Zero Third-Party Data Selling Covenant

We will NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information, vehicle records, or diagnostic history to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers.

Your data is shared strictly with authorized subcontractors only when required to execute repairs (such as specialized machine shops or certified tire distributors) or as mandated by law enforcement and court order pursuant to Ontario statutes.

5. Data Retention & Cloud Security Controls

We maintain appropriate physical, technological, and procedural safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or theft:

  • Statutory Retention Period: Automotive repair work orders, parts receipts, and billing records are retained for a minimum of seven (7) years to comply with the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) and Ontario commercial accounting regulations.
  • Cloud Architecture: Digital appointment requests and intake telemetry are transmitted using industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption and processed through private serverless cloud pipelines.
  • Secure Disposal: Digital records past their statutory retention schedule are permanently deleted, and physical documents are cross-cut shredded.

6. Your Rights Under PIPEDA

As an Ontario consumer, you retain comprehensive rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: You may request a complete transcript of your stored vehicle service records, invoices, and contact data.
  • Right of Rectification: If your contact details or vehicle ownership information has changed, you may request immediate correction.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: You may withdraw consent for optional communication channels (such as SMS or email service reminders) at any time.
